Paper → Product in 24h turns one fresh arXiv paper a day into a startup memo, for founders, product builders, researchers and early-stage investors looking for what's next.

To sponsor a memo or advertise a product, newsletter, event, fund or job opening, email [elkarroumytaha@gmail.com](mailto:elkarroumytaha@gmail.com). Tell us who you are and what you'd like to promote. Sponsors never influence which papers we pick or the verdict, and we won't write a memo on a sponsor's own paper or product without saying so at the top.
